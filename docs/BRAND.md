# Brand Identity Guidelines

**Brand Entities:** Economic Courier / eCS World Wide Express  
**Geographic Market:** Bhilwara, Rajasthan, India  
**Document:** Brand Identity System & Usage Guidelines  
**Status:** Approved for Client Presentation  

---

## 1. Brand Hierarchy & Names

The business operates under a dual brand structure:

1. **Economic Courier (Commercial / Trade Name):**
   - The familiar, trusted local brand known to textile traders, businesses, and individuals across Bhilwara.
   - Represents accessible pricing, dependable daily service, doorstep collection, and friendly local counter staff.
2. **eCS World Wide Express (Express Network & International Identity):**
   - The international courier and express cargo arm.
   - Represents high-speed transit, customs clearance capability, airline cargo partnerships, and overseas reach.

### Recommended Dual Lockup Strategy
On the website header and hero, both names must coexist coherently:
- **Primary Logo:** **eCS** emblem with **World Wide Express** sub-brand.
- **Local Descriptor:** **Economic Courier • Bhilwara Express Hub**.

---

## 2. Brand Positioning & Mission

### 2.1 Mission Statement
> *"To empower Bhilwara's textile commerce and local community with prompt, dependable, and transparent courier logistics across India and over 220 international destinations."*

### 2.2 Brand Pillars
- **Velocity with Care:** Rapid dispatch without compromising parcel safety or package integrity.
- **Textile Trade Understanding:** Specialized comprehension of swatch urgency, fabric sampling cycles, and export documentation requirements.
- **Approachable Ground Support:** A real physical presence in Bhilwara with direct telephone and WhatsApp support rather than an impersonal automated phone tree.

## 4. Logo Usage Guidelines

### 4.1 Safe Space (Clearance Zone)
Maintain an unobstructed clearance zone around the logo equal to the height of the letter **"e"** in the eCS wordmark on all four sides. No text, imagery, or UI borders should encroach within this zone.

### 4.2 Minimum Dimensions
- **Digital Header:** Minimum width `160px` (or height `40px`).
- **Favicon / App Icon:** Minimum `32px × 32px` using the standalone eCS monogram emblem.

### 4.3 Color Variants
- **Positive (Default):** Navy typography with Express Red chevron/accent on pure white or light slate backgrounds.
- **Reverse (Dark Surface):** White typography with Express Red emblem on `#0F172A` or dark footer surfaces.
- **Monochrome:** Approved only for single-color delivery challans, invoices, or stamped packaging.

### 4.4 Logo "Don'ts"
- ❌ Do NOT distort, stretch, or alter the aspect ratio of the eCS logo.
- ❌ Do NOT change the hue of the Express Red or replace it with neon or orange shades.
- ❌ Do NOT place the colored logo over busy background photography without an opaque card backdrop.
- ❌ Do NOT combine unapproved typefaces with the official mark.

## 5. Voice & Tone Guidelines

The communication style must resonate with Bhilwara business owners:

| Dimension | Tone Specification | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Professionalism** | Direct, courteous, authoritative | *"Same-day pickup across Bhilwara industrial sectors."* |
| **Clarity** | Simple, jargon-free logistics terms | *"Express air dispatch with door-to-door overseas delivery."* |
| **Local Grounding** | Aware of Bhilwara's fabric trading rhythm | *"Designed for fabric swatch dispatches and mill sample consignments."* |
| **Integrity** | Honest about tracking and delivery times | *"Consignment status available instantly via our dispatch desk."* |

---

## 6. Imagery & Visual Language

1. **Authentic Operations Over Stock Stereotypes:**
   - Prioritize real photos of the Bhilwara office counter, delivery team, and custom sample packaging.
   - Never use photos of European or North American couriers wearing rival uniforms.
2. **Industrial Context:**
   - Visual motifs should subtly celebrate textile fabrics, quality packaging boxes, clear consignment labels, and modern air cargo freighters.
