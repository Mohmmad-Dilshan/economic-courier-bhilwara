## 2. Master Website Copy Architecture

### 2.1 Hero Section
- **Badge:** `BHILWARA'S TRUSTED LOGISTICS PARTNER`
- **Primary Headline:**
  > **Rapid, Reliable Courier Services Across India & Worldwide**
- **Sub-headline:**
  > Specializing in express fabric sample logistics, domestic priority air freight, and worldwide document dispatch directly from the textile heart of Bhilwara.
- **Action Triggers:**
  - `[Primary Button]` **Book a Pickup via WhatsApp**
  - `[Secondary Button]` **Track Consignment**
  - `[Call Strip]` Direct Call: `+91 [Client Phone]`

### 2.2 Textile Logistics Specialization Block
- **Headline:**
  > **Engineered for Bhilwara’s Textile & Garment Industry**
- **Copy:**
  > Time is of the essence when buyer decisions depend on your fabric swatches. We provide priority sample courier solutions connecting Bhilwara’s weaving and processing mills to buyers across Mumbai, Surat, Delhi NCR, and international fashion capitals with guaranteed speed and tamper-evident packaging.

### 2.3 Core Services Matrix
1. **Domestic Express Freight:**
   - Overnight delivery to key industrial hubs (Delhi, Ahmedabad, Mumbai, Jaipur, Surat).
   - High-priority surface and air connectivity.
2. **eCS Worldwide Express:**
   - Reliable courier transit to 220+ global countries and territories.
   - Comprehensive commercial invoice and customs documentation guidance.
3. **B2B Fabric & Mill Sample Logistics:**
   - Urgent swatch packaging, fabric roll sample delivery, catalog dispatch.
   - Tailored pickup schedules from RIICO industrial zones and city trade offices.
4. **Secure Document & Educational Courier:**
   - University transcripts, legal briefs, passport dossiers, and sensitive deeds.
   - End-to-end protective waterproof sleeves and milestone tracking.

### 2.4 Tracking Section Prototype Copy
- **Headline:**
  > **Consignment Tracking Desk**
- **Sub-text:**
  > Enter your consignment or AWB reference number below to check the real-time movement of your parcel.
- **Input Placeholder:** `e.g. ECS-BHL-8942`
- **Demonstration Notice:**
  > *Notice for Client Review: This is an interactive demo interface. Live real-time API integration with carrier networks will be connected upon franchise system onboarding.*

### 2.5 Quick Rate & Consultation Form Copy
- **Form Headline:** Request a Quick Rate Estimate
- **Fields:**
  - Consignment Type: (Dropdown: Fabric Samples, Documents, Personal Parcel, Commercial Cargo)
  - Origin: Pre-filled *"Bhilwara, Rajasthan"*
  - Destination City / Country: Text input
  - Approx Weight: Dropdown (`< 0.5 kg`, `0.5–2 kg`, `2–5 kg`, `5+ kg`)
  - Contact WhatsApp / Mobile: Number input
- **Submit Action:** *"Send Inquiry via WhatsApp"* (Auto-generates structured WhatsApp text).
